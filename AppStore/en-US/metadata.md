# English (United States)

## Name
Reverse Wiki

## Subtitle
Hidden history of places

## Promotional text
Photograph a place, uncover the story behind the image, and verify it through cited sources and a map.

## Description
What if every place had another story to tell?

Reverse Wiki identifies a monument, street, building, or landscape from your photo. It then challenges the familiar tourist narrative with a lesser-known historical fact and sources you can open yourself.

• Take a photo or import one  
• Use GPS when helpful—it remains optional  
• Choose your AI provider and model  
• Read the verified fact, common story, sources, and map  
• Revisit or delete discoveries in History  
• Share a postcard or a complete PDF with the photo, text, and map

Reverse Wiki supports Gemini, Claude, GPT, Kimi, OpenRouter, and OpenAI-compatible services. Only models that can understand both text and images are displayed.

Try the shared Gemini access, add your own free Gemini API key for more reliable use, or select a paid model. API keys are stored securely in your device Keychain.

AI responses can contain mistakes. Always review the cited sources before sharing a claim.
